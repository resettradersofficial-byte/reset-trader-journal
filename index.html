01 / BEFORE MARKET

Don't enter the market before you enter yourself.

What are you bringing into the market right now? Choose what is present, not what you think you should feel.

Look underneath the obvious answer. Am I here to execute, recover, prove myself, validate my ability, avoid another loss, make money, or get a particular outcome?

Don't just name the emotion. What would the loss make me believe about myself, my trading, or my progress? What might I feel I need to do next?

02 / READINESS

Rate the state, not your confidence.

How activated are you? 1 = neutral, 5 = highly charged.

How strongly do you feel you need to act rather than wait?

How much is today's P&L tied to your emotional state or sense of progress?

Read this as a commitment to action, sizing and process, not a promise about the result.

03 / TRADE LOG

Live entries should take seconds. Deep reflection happens after. Record the decision process separately from the result.

THE 60-SECOND RESET

Use after a loss, missed entry, big win, comment, rule violation, sudden urge, or any moment where the next trade feels emotionally necessary.

02BREATHE4 in · 4 hold · 4 out · 4 hold × 4.
03NAMEIdentify the precise emotion.
04CHECKAm I executing, or trying to change how I feel?
05CHOOSETake the trade only if the setup and rules still qualify.

04 / SESSION REVIEW

Review the behavior, not just the P&L.

Look for a repeatable sequence: trigger → body → emotion → ego → thought → action.

Identify the exact moment you had a chance to pause and choose, or the moment the reaction became the decision.

Record process wins, even when the trade lost.

Choose one behavior you can observe tomorrow. Make it specific enough to know whether you followed it.
